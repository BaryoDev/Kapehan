What changed from the first pass

No perfect circles, and no cup traced twelve times. Contours wobble, fills sit a few pixels off their lines, hatching is a handful of strokes in the shade. Every scene is cropped like a photo.

Placement rules

Squares max out around 240px on a phone and 420px on desktop. The headline carries the message; the doodle sets the mood. Wide scenes bleed to a container edge or sit beside text, never boxed in a card with a border.

Recolour, pause, print

Set --acc on any parent to recolour the coffee; --anim: paused for a still. Downloads bake both in. MIT, like the icons. The repo.
